# 07-negator

**Did nobody attend the meeting?**

Slots: `nobody / attend / the meeting`. Unused: `not`.

`Did` is fixed, `nobody` is the subject, and `attend` is the base verb required after `did`. The question checks the proposition that no one attended, which fits your zero-attendance context.

`Nobody` already expresses negative meaning. Adding `not` could change the meaning toward asking whether no one failed to attend; it would not simply reinforce zero attendance. This frame also has only three slots, and the supplied `nobody`, `attend`, and `the meeting` are all needed for the intended complete question. No wording needs to change.
