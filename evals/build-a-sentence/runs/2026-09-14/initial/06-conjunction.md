# 06-conjunction

**You need to bring your pass with you.**

Slots: `to bring / your pass / with you`. Unused: `because`.

`Need` takes the infinitive phrase `to bring your pass with you`. This tells the person what they need to bring to enter the workshop. `Because` would introduce a reason, but the bank provides no complete reason clause to follow it. It cannot replace one of the three required pieces and produce a complete grammatical reply.

Unused tiles can be conjunctions, negators, or other kinds of words, as well as competing verb forms. Eliminate each piece using the actual frame, grammar, and meaning.
