# 03-subject-question

**Who called the lab?**

Slots: `Who / called / the lab`. Unused: `did`.

Your friend's rule is too broad. Here, `Who` is the subject: it replaces the person who called. A neutral subject question does not require auxiliary `do`. Compare the statement “Someone called the lab” with “Who called the lab?”

“Who did call the lab?” can express emphasis in a different setting, but this frame has only three slots, and the bank supplies `called`, not the base form `call` needed after `did`. The direct answer above preserves every supplied piece it uses.
