# 14-reading-regression

Passage: “The library extended its hours, but weekend borrowing still requires a student card.”

Which paraphrase preserves the passage's meaning?

A. The library is open longer, but students still need a student card to borrow items on weekends.

B. The library is open longer, so a student card is no longer needed for weekend borrowing.

C. The library extended its hours only for people who borrow items on weekends.

Choose A, B, or C and quote the words that support your choice.
