## Title
Campus Rainwater Storage

## TOEFL Domain
Environmental Science

## Tier
T1 (D≈3.02; S=3, W=27)

## Core Thesis
While rainwater stored in barrels supplies a campus garden on dry days, volunteers inspect the barrels weekly to keep lids secure and remove debris.

## Pillar A
Context and use: Dry days → use of the stored rainwater; the source states when the water is used but does not report a water shortage.

## Pillar B
Storage mechanism: Collecting rainwater in barrels → water held for later use on dry days.

## Pillar C
Maintenance purpose: Weekly inspections → intended upkeep of secure lids and removal of debris; the source gives the purpose, not measured inspection results.

## Lexical Bindings
`collects rainwater` → `harvests rainwater`
`stored water` → `retained water supply`
`inspect` → `examine for maintenance needs`
`debris` → `accumulated loose material`
