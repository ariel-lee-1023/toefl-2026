## Title
Planning Venue Change

## Prompt
Write to a classmate who missed a planning meeting. Explain the venue change, give the reason, and ask them to bring the posters.

## My Polished Response
Hi Alex,

Since you missed our planning meeting, I wanted to let you know about a venue change for tomorrow. We'll be in Room 8 instead of Room 4, so please head there when you arrive.

The projector in Room 4 is broken, which is why we've changed rooms. Could you bring the posters with you tomorrow? It would be helpful to have them there so we can check them together.

Let me know if you have any questions about the change. Thanks for your help!

Sam
