# 18-buffer-regression

Tier: T1 (D≈3.02; S=3, W=27). I counted collection, dry-day use, and maintenance as three short subtopics.

**Semantic Anchoring (Domain Metadata)**

Environmental Science

**Prefrontal Abstraction (The Core Thesis)**

While rainwater stored in barrels supplies a campus garden on dry days, volunteers inspect the barrels weekly to keep lids secure and remove debris.

**Associative Evidence Mapping (Logical Architecture)**

- Pillar A: Context and use: Dry days → use of the stored rainwater; the source states when the water is used but does not report a water shortage.
- Pillar B: Storage mechanism: Collecting rainwater in barrels → water held for later use on dry days.
- Pillar C: Maintenance purpose: Weekly inspections → intended upkeep of secure lids and removal of debris; the source gives the purpose, not measured inspection results.

**Lexical Binding (Academic Vocabulary)**

`collects rainwater` → `harvests rainwater`
`stored water` → `retained water supply`
`inspect` → `examine for maintenance needs`
`debris` → `accumulated loose material`
