# 02-interactive

Here is one original Build a Sentence exercise for targeted practice using a textual tile interface.

**Context:** You want to attend the next workshop and need to find out its starting time.

**Fixed frame:** `Do you know [s1] [s2] [s3]?`

| Tile ID | Exact tile |
|---|---|
| t1 | begins |
| t2 | the next workshop |
| t3 | begin |
| t4 | when |

Put one intact tile in each slot and identify the unused tile. Reply with your slot order or the complete question. I'll wait for your attempt.
