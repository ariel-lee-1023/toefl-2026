## revised-16-exhibition

**The exhibit about ancient coins opens on Friday.**

- Slot order: s1=t3, s2=t5, s3=t4, s4=t1.
- Unused: t2, `open`.

`Exhibit` is the singular head of the subject. `About ancient coins` modifies it, so plural `coins` does not determine the main verb. The singular form `opens` is required. `On Friday` gives the opening day requested in the context.

## revised-16-bicycle

**Who repaired the bicycle?**

- Slot order: s1=t3, s2=t1.
- Unused: t2, `did`.

`Who` is the subject of this neutral subject question. It is followed directly by finite verb `repaired` and object `the bicycle`. No auxiliary is needed. Inserting `did` would also require the base form `repair`, which is not supplied.
