# Original practice: library tool lending

Original expanded Read in Daily Life learning set, created 2026-09-14. Five questions exceed the usual two- or three-question daily-life sample set; this is not an exam simulation. Design difficulty: moderate. The complete source stays visible and unchanged. The reviewed key is in a separate file, not interleaved here.

## Passage

Starting Monday, the campus library will lend small repair tools to students. Borrowers must reserve a kit online and collect it at the service desk before six in the evening. Each loan lasts three days. Although students may request an extension, staff will approve it only if nobody else has reserved the kit. Kits must be returned to the desk; the outdoor book slot is unsuitable because it could damage the tools. The library will review demand after one month before deciding whether to buy additional kits.

## Questions

1. What is the main purpose of the notice?

   A. To recruit staff for a new repair service
   B. To explain a new service for borrowing tools
   C. To announce new rules for borrowing books
   D. To invite donations of used repair equipment

2. Which requirement applies before a student collects a kit?

   A. The student must pay for replacement tools.
   B. The student must request a loan extension.
   C. The student must register for a repair class.
   D. The student must reserve the kit online.

3. Which statement about extensions is supported?

   A. An extension cannot be approved while another reservation exists.
   B. Every student automatically receives an extension after three days.
   C. Extensions are available only during the service's first month.
   D. Reserving online guarantees approval of a requested extension.

4. Why does the notice mention the outdoor book slot?

   A. To identify where students should collect reserved kits
   B. To describe how library staff will repair damaged tools
   C. To explain why borrowers must return kits to the desk
   D. To announce that the library will remove the book slot

5. What can be inferred about buying more kits?

   A. The library has already committed to doubling its supply.
   B. The decision will take observed demand into account.
   C. Students must buy kits before borrowing the library's tools.
   D. Additional kits will arrive before the service begins.
