**Original script-based conversation practice.** This expanded learning set has three questions and an estimated moderate difficulty. It is text-assisted practice.

> **Maya:** Shall we finish the poster in the library after lunch? I booked a table there.
>
> **Leo:** We can, but the large printer is unavailable until tomorrow. The media lab has one we can use today.
>
> **Maya:** Then let's work there. Do we need to book it?
>
> **Leo:** Yes, and we'll need a staff member to approve our printing request. I'll email the lab coordinator now.
>
> **Maya:** Great. I'll cancel the library booking once the coordinator confirms that we can use the lab.

**1. Why do they decide to use the media lab?**

A. A library staff member moved their reservation there.
B. Its large printer is available today.
C. It offers free poster design lessons.
D. It will remain open later tomorrow.

**2. What will Leo do next?**

A. Cancel the table reservation.
B. Collect the finished poster.
C. Email the lab coordinator.
D. Repair the library printer.

**3. When will Maya cancel the library booking?**

A. Once lab access has been confirmed.
B. As soon as they finish lunch.
C. When the library printer is repaired.
D. After they collect their printed poster.
