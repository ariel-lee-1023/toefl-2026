# Behavioral forward-test results

Method: Loaded the requested SKILL.md and only the relevant task references, then executed each of the nine scenario prompts as a learner-facing response. No expected-output file, worked-interaction example, prior evaluation report, or hidden key was consulted. Files 01 through 09 preserve the generated responses verbatim. Each scenario used only its supplied learner facts; scenario continuity was not assumed. This was one agent producing independently scoped scenarios, not nine separately spawned model sessions.

Result: All nine responses handled their supplied scenario without a material observed failure.

1. Guided notes: Begins an original, clearly labelled script exercise immediately. Invites shorthand notes and waits. No intake gate or Buffer schema.
2. Notes review: Preserves learner notes verbatim, separates revised labels, identifies role mismatch and reported timeout, uses cautious causal language, and offers one retrieval variation. No auditory diagnosis.
3. Announcement generation: States no audio generation or playback, supplies a script rather than claiming a recording, and gives one unspoiled question. This does not complete playable listening generation, which the scenario host cannot do.
4. Next action: Direct answer distinguishes now/check-and-reserve from the revised later outing. No invented vocal evidence or motives.
5. Choose a Response: Explicitly script-based, exactly four choices, one suitable response, no visible first-turn key.
6. Error explanation: Directly explains why a detail is not the main idea, identifies the shared point, and gives one fresh transfer question without its answer.
7. Speaking chunking: Uses two chunks, preserves all source words, does not substitute inferred prepositions, and does not fabricate audio assessment or score. Text-assisted recall is labelled. The request was limited to chunking; the response consequently avoids a full set score or archive.
8. Score request: Refuses to convert five-replay success into an official score, acknowledges review learning, and proposes fresh, once-only progress evidence.
9. Conversation batch: Provides three questions, labels the expanded/script-assisted format, and supplies a separate linked key artifact. The key is not interleaved with the questions.

Observed limitations: These are single-turn textual behaviors. No learner follow-up was supplied, so adaptive feedback after the proposed exercises has not been exercised. Real audio delivery, acoustic verification, live note retrieval time, and playback-first sequencing were intentionally unavailable in these scenarios. The tests do not establish those features work.

Potential maintenance issue, distinguished from observed failure: The Speaking reference's six-step mandatory workflow is broader than a simple chunking request, and its instruction to never show text before a blind attempt conflicts with a user who explicitly supplies and asks to chunk that text. The actual response handled this by honoring the narrower explicit request and labelling assistance. Consider explicitly stating that text-supplied chunking requests may skip blind playback and scoring. No failure was observed in this run.
