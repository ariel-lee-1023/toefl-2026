Original skill-building practice: choose the sentence that preserves the meaning.

**Source:** Students can borrow a laptop for an extra day only if no one else has reserved it.

A. A laptop reservation allows students to borrow it for an additional day.
B. Students must return every laptop a day early if someone reserves it.
C. Keeping a laptop for an additional day requires that nobody else has reserved it.
D. Students who have no reservation cannot borrow a laptop.

Which option matches the source? Reply with a letter and, if you like, the phrase that helped you decide.
