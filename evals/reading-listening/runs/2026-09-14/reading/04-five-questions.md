Original questions on your supplied, familiar passage. This is an expanded learning set of five questions.

> The library moved its weekend workshops online because the main hall needs repairs. Weekday workshops still meet in the library. Members who cannot join live may request a recording, but it will be available only for seven days.

1. What is the main purpose of the notice?

   A. To invite members to help repair the library
   B. To explain workshop arrangements and recording access
   C. To announce the cancellation of all library workshops
   D. To describe the topics covered in upcoming workshops

2. Why did the library move weekend workshops online?

   A. Members preferred watching recordings to attending live.
   B. The library needed space for new weekday workshops.
   C. Online workshops could remain available for longer.
   D. The main hall required repair work.

3. Which arrangement continues at the library?

   A. Workshops held during the week
   B. Live workshops held on weekends
   C. All workshops for library members
   D. Screenings of workshop recordings

4. What does the notice say about members unable to attend live?

   A. They must attend a weekday workshop instead.
   B. They will receive a recording automatically.
   C. They are permitted to request a recording.
   D. They can request a recording only after seven days.

5. In “it will be available only for seven days,” what does “it” refer to?

   A. The main hall
   B. A workshop recording
   C. A weekday workshop
   D. The library
