I’m using the TOEFL skill’s Flexible Mode. Here is a generic weekly plan; it does not assume a score, cognitive profile, or particular weakness. The schedule is my coaching synthesis, not an ETS requirement.

| Session | Practice | Review target |
|---|---|---|
| Monday: Reading, 30 minutes | Use one short academic text and one practical notice. Answer before feedback, then identify the evidence for each choice. | Check whether your paraphrase preserves conditions, negation, and certainty. |
| Tuesday: Listening, 30 minutes | Use an accessible recording without a visible transcript on the first listen. Answer afterward, then review the relevant segment with text if available. | Separate a sound you missed from a meaning you misunderstood; label replay and transcript assistance. |
| Wednesday: Writing, 30 minutes | Complete a small Build a Sentence set, then draft an Email addressing every requested action. | Check syntax against context and the email’s elaboration and recipient-appropriate tone. |
| Thursday: Speaking, 25 minutes | Use actual audio for Listen and Repeat, then answer a four-question Interview set at 45 seconds per answer. | For repetition, compare exact words and intelligibility. For Interview, assess relevance, examples, and recorded delivery. |
| Friday: Writing and transfer, 30 minutes | Write an Academic Discussion contribution, then revisit one earlier Reading or Listening relationship using fresh material. | Add an idea beyond the student posts; distinguish remembered answers from new transfer. |
