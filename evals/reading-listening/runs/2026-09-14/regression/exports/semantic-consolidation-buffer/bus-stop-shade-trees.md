## Title
Bus Stop Shade Trees

## TOEFL Domain
Environmental Studies

## Tier
T1 (S=3, W=31, D=3.021)

## Core Thesis
While volunteers watered young shade trees through their first season, the town’s bus-stop planting programme aims to reduce summer heat and will undergo a passenger-comfort comparison next summer before expansion.

## Pillar A
Summer heat → the town planted shade trees near bus stops with the aim of reducing that heat.

## Pillar B
The young-tree planting programme → volunteer watering through the first season; the source reports this support but does not establish its effect on survival or cooling.

## Pillar C
Planned expansion → passenger comfort will be compared next summer beforehand; neither improved comfort nor a completed expansion is reported.
