## Title
Joining a Full Seminar

## Prompt
Draft an email to a professor asking to join a full seminar, explain your interest in urban history, and offer to attend if a place opens.

## My Polished Response
Dear Professor,

I am writing to ask whether I could join your urban history seminar if a place becomes available. I understand that the class is currently full, and I would appreciate being considered for the waiting list.

I am particularly interested in how changes in housing and transport have shaped city neighborhoods. Exploring these developments would help me connect historical decisions with the streets and communities we see today.

If another student withdraws, I would be happy to attend and catch up on any material I have missed. Please let me know whether there is a waiting-list procedure I should follow.

Thank you for considering my request.

Kind regards,
[Your Name]
