# Baking Cookies

## Prompt
1. Begin by mixing the butter and sugar.
2. Spread the dough so the cookies bake evenly.
3. Preheat the oven to the appropriate temperature.
4. Evenly space the cookie shapes onto the baking sheet.
5. Insert the tray and bake until the cookie edges are golden.
6. After the baking time is complete, cool the cookies on the rack for 5 minutes.
7. Pour yourself a glass of milk, and enjoy cookies fresh out of the oven.
