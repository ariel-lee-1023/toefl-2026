# Campus Dining Hall Duties

## Prompt
1. Check your schedule to confirm your work time.
2. Arrive on time and ready to start your shift.
3. Learn the menu items at your station before service begins.
4. Use separate utensils for each dish during service.
5. Maintain a friendly attitude when serving guests in the line.
6. When food service ends, you will need to stay 30 more minutes to help clean up.
7. If you need time off, speak to your supervisor so your shift can be covered.
