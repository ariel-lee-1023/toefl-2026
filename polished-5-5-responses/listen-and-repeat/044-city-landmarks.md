# City Landmarks

## Prompt
Scenario: You are working a summer internship for the city. Your supervisor is teaching you how to provide tourists information about city landmarks. Listen to the supervisor and repeat what the supervisor says. Repeat only once.

1. This castle is famous for its towers.
2. The statue celebrates important events.
3. Visit the market to buy local goods and fresh produce.
4. The park is perfect for relaxing and outdoor activities.
5. See art displays and historical relics at the museum.
6. Guided city tours can provide detailed information about each landmark.
7. When needed, check out the city map for directions and transportation options.
