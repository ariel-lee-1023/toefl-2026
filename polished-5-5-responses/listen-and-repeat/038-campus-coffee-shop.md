# Campus Coffee Shop

## Prompt
Scenario: You are being trained to assist in a campus coffee shop. Your supervisor will teach you how to explain key features of the coffee shop to customers. Listen to your supervisor and repeat what the supervisor says. Repeat only once.

1. We serve coffee and tea at the main counter.
2. Our pastries are all made fresh daily.
3. The menu board lists drinks that include a range of herbal teas.
4. Milk, cream, and sugar are available at the station.
5. Dispose of trash and recyclables in the bins near the door.
6. Some tables in the seating area offer excellent views of campus.
7. Free Wi-Fi is available for campus visitors as well as students.
