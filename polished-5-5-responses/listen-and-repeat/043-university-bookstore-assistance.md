# University Bookstore Assistance

## Prompt
Scenario: You are working at your university's bookstore. Your supervisor is training you to assist customers in the bookstore. Listen to the supervisor and repeat what the supervisor says. Repeat only once.

1. All our books are located on the shelves.
2. Bring your purchases to the cash register.
3. Use the inventory to search for available items.
4. Our staff provides a monthly list of recommendations.
5. Information about membership benefits is displayed here.
6. We have many interesting store events that we recommend attending.
7. We value customer feedback, so please consider leaving a store review.
