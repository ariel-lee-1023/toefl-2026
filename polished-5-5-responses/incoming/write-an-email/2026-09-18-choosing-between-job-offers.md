## Title
Choosing Between Job Offers

## Prompt
Your friend Priya recently graduated from university and has received two job offers. One is at a large corporation with a higher salary, and the other is at a small startup closer to her family. She is having difficulty deciding and has asked for your advice.

Write an email to Priya. In your email, do the following:
- Describe the advantages of each option.
- Share your recommendation and explain your reasoning.
- Suggest how she might make her final decision.

Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

To: Priya
Subject: Job offer advice

## My Polished Response
Hi Priya,

Congratulations on both offers! I can see why you're finding it hard to choose.

The corporation offers a higher salary and may provide structured training, while the startup would keep you closer to family and could let you try a wider range of tasks.

I'd lean toward the corporation as a first step. Learning how different departments work together could give you a solid understanding of how a business operates. You could also build a professional network that would be useful if you eventually started your own company.

Before deciding, ask both employers about training and typical working hours. Then rank your priorities, including income, learning opportunities, and family time, and compare the offers against them. If being near family matters most right now, the startup may be the better fit.

Let me know what you decide!

Best,
Ariel
