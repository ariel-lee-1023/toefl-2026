## Title
Birdwatching Nature Center

## Prompt
Scenario: You are volunteering at a community nature center near campus. The leader is training you to help beginners learn the basics of birdwatching. Listen to the leader and repeat what the leader says. Repeat only once.

1. Look towards the treetops where you can find nests.
2. Note details to help identify the bird.
3. Keep a record of each sighting to share later with the group.
4. Use proper footwear to keep from slipping while exploring outdoors.
5. Keep your head covered to protect yourself from the sun on the walk.
6. When getting ready for a day out, be sure to pack essentials to be prepared.
7. If you spot a bird nearby, avoid sudden movements so it does not fly away.
