# Library Printer Repair Request

## Prompt
You are a student employee at the university library and have discovered that the printer keeps malfunctioning. You need to request assistance from the IT department to resolve the problem.

Write an email to the IT support team. In your email, do the following:

- Describe the issues you have been experiencing with the printer.
- Explain how these issues are impacting library users.
- Ask for a technician to visit and fix the printer as soon as possible.

Write as much as you can and in complete sentences. Use your own words.

To: IT Support Team
Subject: Library printer malfunction issues

## My Polished Response
Dear IT Support Team,

I am a student employee at the university library, and I am writing to request assistance with our printer. It frequently fails to connect via Bluetooth or Wi-Fi and sometimes stops responding after scanning a document.

These problems prevent students from printing assignments before class and delay instructors who need copies of teaching materials. Users have to wait or find another printer, which disrupts their schedules and creates frustration.

Could you please arrange for a technician to visit the library and repair the printer as soon as possible? Please let me know when someone can come so that I can be available to demonstrate the problems.

Thank you for your help.

Sincerely,
Ariel
